About Zanokusha Enterprise

Our Brief History

Zanokusha Enterprise is a family business established in Ndlangubo, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Founded by the Mkhwanazi family with a vision to bring locally sourced products to the marketplace, our enterprise began as a community-based business selling vegetables to local residents.

Through dedication and quality service, we have grown from serving our community to becoming a large-scale supplier for local supermarkets and businesses across the KwaZulu-Natal region. Our success is built on trust, quality, and a commitment to supporting local agriculture.

Our Mission Statement

"Zanokusha Enterprise's mission is to demonstrate to the people of South Africa and to KZN citizens that agriculture should remain the leading industry in terms of providing sustained jobs to mainly young and unemployed people. The company's sole mission is to create job opportunities and empower communities through agricultural excellence."

Our Vision Statement

"Zanokusha Enterprise's vision is to lead the transformation of modern agriculture by cultivating high-yield, sustainable farming practices that nourish communities while restoring the earth for future generations."

Our Core Values

  • Quality: We maintain the highest standards in our agricultural products
  • Sustainability: We practice environmentally responsible farming methods
  • Community: We are dedicated to creating job opportunities and supporting local communities
  • Integrity: We conduct our business with honesty and transparency
  • Innovation: We continuously improve our practices and embrace modern agricultural techniques
